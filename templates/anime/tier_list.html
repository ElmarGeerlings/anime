Elmar's tier list

Tiering is based on how much I enjoyed the anime, rather than how good I think it is. Anime that are rated lower are thus not necessarily bad, I just had less strong emotions tied to it. In fact, I think almost all of these anime are quite good.

I tried to rate the anime based on my feelings at the time I watched it. There's probably some shows that I would rate rather differently if I would have (re-)watched them now.

GOAT tier is a bit special, since I find it hard to compare these iconic anime with hundreds of episodes to all the others that have way less. In that sense they are in a tier of itself.

The anime marked with a cyan border are still ongoing.

You are invited to rate these anime yourself and contribute to the Visitor's tier list!
